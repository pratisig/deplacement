Dot size shows all households that moved into, out of or within the district. Line thickness shows households on that route. Hover a dot to see only its routes.

In Network layout, districts that exchange many households sit close together, so distance has no geographic meaning. Switch to Map to place each district at its centre point on a real map of Yemen, with district limits (Natural Earth ADM2) and governorate limits (geoBoundaries ADM1). The Limits button switches between the two or hides them.

Background maps are public tile servers that need no API key and no sign-up: Esri dark gray, Esri light gray, Esri satellite, OpenTopoMap, Wikimedia Maps, OpenStreetMap France and OpenStreetMap. If a server refuses the tiles, the map switches to the next one by itself.

Source: IOM DTM Rapid Displacement Tracking, district level. Households are counted from assessments and added up from 4 January to the date chosen, so a family assessed twice counts twice.
